# Campaign text plate rule

Apply this rule to review-only Accelerated raster campaign layouts and future revisions of this package.

1. Measure the rendered heading and body in their final fonts. Wrap them inside the available placement lane, then size the bordered plate to the measured lines. Use 24–32px internal padding on each side; this generator uses 28px.
2. Short copy gets a compact plate. Add height or width only when the copy requires it. Keep the title and body together with a modest gap, and keep the plate clear of the footer.
3. Place plates in open visual fields. Preserve the scale and complete geometry of the canonical Signals, Alignment, and Momentum sources. Keep labels, faces, and product controls unobstructed.
4. Check every square carousel at 1080×1080 and in a 390×844 target-size composition. Check vertical frames at 1080×1920, wide frames at 1920×1080, and the thumbnail at 1280×720. Reject clipping, crowded text, unreadable body copy, covered source details, and large empty plate interiors.
5. Keep the approved words, CTA, source hashes, original review receipt, and public campaign assets fixed. Write revisions and their SHA-256 receipt to a separate review directory. Review artifacts do not authorize upload, scheduling, publishing, posting, provider calls, outreach, or spend.
