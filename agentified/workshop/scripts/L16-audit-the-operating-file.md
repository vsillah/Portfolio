## Exercise and model Operating File entry

**Prompt:** Complete an `Accountability Stack` for the workflow you designed across L01–L15. For each of source, event, proposal, memory, routing, action, approval, cost/spend, export, and rollback, record owner, evidence reference, privacy tier, approval gate, failure mode, and next improvement. Identify the weakest gate. Add a disposition with `keep`, `shrink`, `retire`, or `pending`; named human owner; decision date; reason; next review date; and unresolved evidence. If the human has not decided, leave date blank and status `pending`. This exercise does not authorize a run.

**Harbor example — `SYN-AUDIT-01`:** Source: three invented inputs with IDs only; owner Morgan; missing real-use permission review. Event: paper receipt shape `SYN-TRACE-01`; no actual run. Proposal: Session Option Brief and `SYN-RESEARCH-01`, the latter `not_run`. Memory: staffing and source-status rules `needs_review`, with no promotion. Routing: preparation agent drafts; Morgan receives the held brief; missing owner escalates to Morgan rather than an automatic action. Action: paper correction only; booking, announcement, contact, and purchase excluded. Approval: `SYN-HOLD-01` staffing confirmation pending; session decision unexercised. Cost/spend: `SYN-SPEND-01` unapproved; no incurred cost. Export: `SYN-PROOF-01` `not shared`, privacy review pending. Rollback: preserve prior paper version and record correction; any future local rule must have a revoke owner. Privacy tier: invented classroom data throughout; no raw private records. Weakest gate: staffing evidence and named confirmation. Suggested disposition for Morgan: `shrink` to a paper brief correction and staffing check; **actual disposition: `pending`**, owner Morgan, decision date blank, reason “awaiting staffing confirmation and review,” next review date blank until Morgan sets it. The foundry idea remains unscored. No numeric capstone score is invented.

**Claim map for source review:** Route/log/gate/memory/review and the weekly accountability rhythm derive from Chapter 26. The ten worksheet layers, owner/evidence/privacy/gate/failure/next-improvement prompts, and rollback concern derive from workbook “Accountability Stack.” Keep/shrink/retire is the workshop's human portfolio disposition carried from earlier lessons. Harbor's suggested shrink path is fictional and explicitly unapproved.
