## Exercise and model Operating File entry

**Prompt:** Write an `AutoResearch Proposal` for one improvement to your invented workflow. Include title, question, hypothesis, comparison, expected impact, baseline, primary and guardrail metrics, test configuration, touched surface, risk, rollback, promotion recommendation, named approval question, next metric gate, `result_status: not_run`, and an actionable hold reason. The approval question must authorize only a later scoped research action, never a production change.

**Harbor example — `SYN-RESEARCH-01`:** Question: would a staffing-first Session Option Brief help Morgan find the unresolved staffing issue more reliably? Hypothesis: on invented paper cases, a reviewer will identify `SYN-HOLD-01` at least as accurately with the staffing-first layout and may find it faster. Baseline: `unknown`; no timed review has been performed. Proposed comparison: two fictional layouts with identical synthetic facts; review protocol and participant permission remain to be designed. Primary metric: correct identification of the staffing hold. Guardrail: no source or uncertainty label may disappear. Expected direction: at least equal accuracy; no numeric lift promised. Touched surface: paper brief layout; no live settings or files. Risk: a prominent staffing box could hide source uncertainty. Rollback: retain the existing paper layout. Promotion recommendation: none. Approval question: “Morgan, should a separate, bounded paper review protocol be drafted for your approval?” Next metric gate: define a baseline and guardrail threshold before any test. Result: `not_run`. Hold reason: no agreed comparison set, reviewer protocol, or permission. `SYN-SPEND-01` remains unapproved, and the foundry idea remains unscored.

**Claim map for source review:** The propose/`not_run`/gate/trace/disposition ladder, required metric gates, rollback, and named surface owner derive from Chapter 23. Baseline, touched settings, risk, approval question, and next metric gate derive from workbook “AutoResearch Proposal.” Harbor's layout hypothesis and IDs are fictional, with no measured result or actual experiment.

**Accessibility:** Caption “baseline unknown” and “not run” exactly. Supply a linear reading order for proposal fields and a text alternative for the ladder. Use an explicit hold label, not red alone. Keep the exercise timer pausable and check caption clearance and mobile text size in a later prototype.

**Editorial hold:** Verify that the fictional hypothesis is never reported as a result and that the proposed test, memory promotion, staffing, and spend each retain separate human gates. No live execution, provider call, media production, or external sharing is authorized; the proposal is `not shared`.
