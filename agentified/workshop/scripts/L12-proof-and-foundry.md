## Exercise and model Operating File entry

**Prompt:** Create a `Client-Safe Proof Packet` outline for one fictional Harbor decision with audience, scope, window, capability and delegation boundary, trace references, approval/hold history, spend authority summary, exclusions, export review owner, and ledger metadata. Then create a `Foundry Test` for one adjacent app idea. For each field, name its evidence or mark it `unscored`; include demand, payer/monetization, builder fit, build velocity, differentiation, release readiness, approval gates, the smallest proposed test, and stop condition. Do not substitute a fabricated total score for missing evidence.

**Harbor example — Client-Safe Proof Packet:** Audience: fictional board reviewer. Scope: one Session Option Brief paper exercise. Window: one invented Friday planning cycle. Include: prepare-only capability, source IDs without raw content, handoff contract `paper-v1`, `SYN-TRACE-01` reference, cases A/B/C as classroom test labels, staffing hold `SYN-HOLD-01`, Morgan's unexercised decision, and `SYN-SPEND-01` marked unapproved. Exclude: raw notes, prompts, private reasoning, personal details, unrelated work, and any real customer data. Export: outline for Morgan's review only, status `not shared`. Ledger: proposed packet ID `SYN-PROOF-01`, audience, scope, window, exclusions, reviewer, and draft timestamp label; no actual export event is claimed. This is proof of the paper design's stated limits, not proof of production performance.

**Harbor example — Foundry Test:** Idea `SYN-IDEA-01`: a volunteer coordinator phone tool for comparing possible free workshop sessions. Demand: `unscored`, no attributable external signal. Monetization/payer: `unscored`, payer unknown. Builder fit: `unscored`, no team capability review. Build velocity: `unscored`, no scoped estimate. Differentiation: `unscored`, no comparator set. Release readiness: `unscored`, no privacy or distribution review. Approval gates: Morgan would have to approve a bounded research plan; later repo creation, tester invite, data collection, pricing, and publication each require separate decisions. Smallest proposed test: prepare a public-source research brief for review, with no contact or provider call. Stop: if payer and user needs remain unsupported, shelve the idea. The workbook's 25/20/20/15/10/10 weighting is documented but no numeric score is produced without attributable evidence.

**Claim map for source review:** Audience, scope, window, exclusions, export, ledger, and human review before sending derive from Chapter 21; capability, delegation, cost authority, and reviewer-note prompts derive from workbook “Client-Safe Proof Packet.” The seven Foundry fields and score/packet/approve/build order derive from Chapter 22; weighting and forbidden pre-build actions derive from workbook “Foundry Test.” Harbor proof and app idea are fictional; no source claims a real board request, product demand, or release readiness.

**Avatar/B-roll:** Approved presenter may introduce the board request and the build temptation. Full-frame proof and foundry cards carry the actual teaching. No provider call or media generation is authorized; avoid real client, board, product, trace, or app footage and unreviewed art.

**Pronunciation:** Say “foundry” as “FOWN-dree” and `SYN-PROOF-01` as “the sample proof outline.” Read “unscored” clearly. Do not voice source paths or raw IDs.

**Accessibility:** Provide linear text alternatives for the scoped packet and seven-field card. State `not shared`, `unapproved`, and `unscored` aloud and in captions; do not encode them through color alone. Keep the timer pausable and verify text size, reading order, and caption clearance in a later prototype.

**Editorial hold:** Reviewers confirm that the proof outline cannot be mistaken for an export or certification and that an unscored idea never receives build authority. No raw data access, provider execution, media generation, external send, or publication is authorized.
