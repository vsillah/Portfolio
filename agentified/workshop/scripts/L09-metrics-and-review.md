## Exercise and model Operating File entry

**Prompt:** Build an `Agent Metrics Ladder` with activity, quality, trust or behavior, cost, outcome, and one metric excluded from decisions. For each retained measure, add evidence source, cadence, action threshold, decision, and owner; mark missing baselines `unproven`. Build `Review Postures` for low, medium, and high-risk actions: posture, reviewer evidence, approve/revise/reject/escalate conditions, and trace pointer. Do not infer real performance from the classroom cases.

**Harbor example — Agent Metrics Ladder:** Activity: number of `SYN-BRIEF-01` drafts, diagnostic only. Quality: share of claims supported by current permitted `SYN-ROOM-01`, `SYN-ATTEND-01`, and `SYN-FAC-01`; any unsupported staffing claim triggers revision. Behavior/trust: correct hold on stale or missing evidence; any missed hold stops the paper design for Morgan's review. Cost: illustrative preparation and Morgan review minutes, with no actual cost or savings claim. Outcome: whether Morgan can identify a defensible option and the open question; remains `unproven` until an approved larger evaluation. Learning: retest the same error after the contract changes; no improvement claim yet. Authority: `prepare only`, held constant. Review cadence: after each invented case, with a later cadence requiring human approval. Owner: Morgan. Excluded vanity metric: raw draft count as proof of value.

**Harbor example — Review Postures:** Low risk, drafting `SYN-BRIEF-01`: prepare-only paper action; Morgan samples source and claim alignment before using it. Medium risk, Morgan's internal proposal to the team: pre-action human review with full brief, source status, alternatives, trace `SYN-TRACE-01`, and visible hold/revise choices. High risk, external announcement or booking: separate explicit human authority and full context; blocked in this course. A stale `SYN-FAC-01` forces `SYN-HOLD-01`; Morgan may request a new cleared note or leave the proposal on hold. No send, booking, or publication is performed.

**Claim map for source review:** Seven rungs and the link from measure to human authority decision derive from Chapter 15. Activity, quality, trust, cost, outcome, cadence, and a discarded metric derive from the workbook's “Agent Metrics Ladder.” Pre-action review, bounded action, post-action audit, signer view, and side-effect gate derive from Chapter 16; review instructions and disposition choices derive from “Review Postures.” Harbor thresholds and statuses are invented design examples, not observed results.
