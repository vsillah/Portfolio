## Exercise and model Operating File entry

**Prompt:** Draft a `Spend Envelope` for a fictional proposed expense and a `Decision Brief` for the Harbor session choice. The envelope must identify exact amount and currency, counterparty, purpose, execution window, retry limit, source trace, requesting role, approving human, expiry/revocation, and forbidden actions. The brief must offer at least two fairly argued options, the case against its recommendation, reversibility, what would change the recommendation, owner, deadline, and an explicit `hold` disposition. Neither document grants authority.

**Harbor example — Spend Envelope:** `SYN-SPEND-01`, status `unapproved classroom proposal`; amount USD 40.00 exactly; fictional counterparty Harbor Print Shop; purpose, print a small flyer test for a possible free session; proposed window, two invented calendar days after a separate signed approval; retries, zero; trace `SYN-TRACE-01` and draft `SYN-BRIEF-01`; requester, paper preparation role; approver, Morgan; expiry, end of proposed window; revocation, Morgan withdraws the paper proposal if staffing, terms, or evidence changes. Forbidden: ordering, card entry, budget increase, subscription, paid API call, or outreach. There is no actual quote or payment instruction.

**Harbor example — Decision Brief:** Context: Friday planning for one possible free session; room and attendance notes are current, `SYN-FAC-01` remains unconfirmed. Option A: conditionally propose a session for internal discussion while making staffing uncertainty prominent; benefit is earlier planning, risk is confusion if the condition is lost. Option B: hold the proposal until a facilitator commitment is documented; benefit is a cleaner promise, cost is a later decision. Recommendation: B until a current permitted staffing note supports A. Reversibility: the internal paper proposal can be withdrawn; a public promise would be harder to undo and is outside scope. What would change our mind: a current, cleared facilitator confirmation and Morgan's separate decision. Owner: Morgan. Deadline: the next fictional planning review. Evidence gap: staffing. Disposition: `SYN-HOLD-01`; mock print envelope remains unapproved regardless of the session disposition.

**Claim map for source review:** The seven core envelope fields and separate money authority come from Chapter 17; extra ceiling and forbidden-action prompts come from workbook “Spend Envelope.” The five brief sections, equal-weight alternative, and human signature come from Chapter 18; owner, deadline, evidence gap, and rollback prompts come from workbook “Decision Brief.” Harbor amount, vendor, and options are invented classroom material.
