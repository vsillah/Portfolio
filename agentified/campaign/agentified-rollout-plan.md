## Strategy

A product leader can ask an agent to draft a useful brief today. The harder question is whether another person can inspect the source, boundary, review gate, and result before acting. This campaign turns that tension into an invitation to practice with the Agentified workshop.

| Value-equation lever | Campaign expression | Proof to show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Desired outcome | Useful, governed agents that can help with real work | A bounded job and a human decision in the workshop exercises |
| Likelihood | The workshop, Operating File, receipts, permission slips, review gates, proof surfaces, and credential path make practice inspectable | Public workshop UI and approved lesson captures; credential claim must be rechecked before publication |
| Time to value | A public guest can start learning immediately | `/agentified` and `/agentified/learningplan` |
| Effort | Sign-in is optional; thirteen guided-video lessons and three transcript-led lessons have working pages | Current public page and lesson UI |

Do not convert the equation into a numerical outcome, guarantee, implied certification, testimonial, countdown, seat limit, or scarcity claim. A credential is a reviewable learning artifact/path, not a job qualification promise.

## Audience and channel roles

LinkedIn is the primary conversation with product leaders, operators, founders, and teams assigning agents real work. Instagram carousels teach one boundary at a time; Reels carry the spoken version. YouTube shows screen-backed public workshop proof and distributes Shorts. Owned email offers the same public learning invitation to an existing, permissioned list only after a separate send approval. X receives optional manual cutdowns only; there is no paid API, scheduling, or provider path. Personalized outreach is a separate campaign and is excluded from this packet.
