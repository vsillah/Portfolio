Twenty wars and campaigns, 1775 to 2026

Home Front Ledger

What each American war cost and what it changed at home, scored on seven dimensions. The numbers link to their sources. The blue and red verdicts are an interpretation of the history, and the scoring rules are written out at the bottom.

Dimensions

Every war against every dimension

Each cell is a judgment call. A plus is a net gain, a minus a net loss, plus-minus is mixed, and a dot means little effect either way. A question mark means the conflict is too recent to score, so the entry lists what is documented so far and leaves the verdict open.

    • Deaths count US military personnel only. Civil War figures cover both sides. Where a source gives an all-sides estimate, it appears under the entry and is left out of the running total.
    • Costs through 1991 are military operations only, taken from the Congressional Research Service in 2011 dollars and restated in 2025 dollars with the Consumer Price Index (a factor of 1.434). CRS used defense price indexes after 1940, so the restated figures are approximate.
    • Post-9/11 costs are Brown University's war-zone estimates in the dollars of the years spent. Brown's full $8 trillion also counts homeland security, interest, and $2.2 trillion in future veterans' care, which this ledger leaves out.
    • The War on Drugs is a domestic campaign with military elements. Its cost joins the running total and it has no death count.
    • Three entries have no cost. The Indian Wars and the Philippine-American War are missing from the CRS table, and the Venezuela strikes have no published estimate, so the running cost is an undercount. The war against ISIS is counted inside the Iraq figure.
    • Recent conflicts (Venezuela and Iran) rest on news reports and early tallies that are still being revised. Only executive power is scored for them, on the rule under How the verdicts are scored.
    • What is left out. Smaller interventions such as Grenada, Lebanon, Somalia, Haiti and Bosnia, the early naval wars, and drone campaigns in Yemen, Somalia and Pakistan are not separate entries.
    • Confidence tags mark each figure as an official count, a scholarly estimate, or a rough estimate.

    Figures checked November 11, 2026.
