Owning the Block

A cross-section of what we are building, read bottom-up. The plumbing nobody sees carries the data. The foundation is the models and the machines. The buildings are the tools people walk into. The street is the network, and the sky is what a network produces. Click any structure to trace what it stands on.

Narration

Start at the street

Every building exists to put more people on the street and keep them there. Click a building to see which pipes feed it and which data it stands on; click a cistern to see which buildings would go dark without it.

Blue is owned by the collective. Gold is open source we can rebuild anywhere. Red is rented and moving out.

How to read it

Pipes are n8n workflows and APIs. Cisterns are stores of data. A building with no pipe to a cistern is a façade. The percent figures are a rough estimate toward the owned end state, not toward a product launch.

Ownership rule

Own the software and the knowledge; rent the machines when you must. Everything in the dashed tower has a self-hosted or open-weight replacement already identified.
