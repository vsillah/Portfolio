## Funnel and qualification

Track aggregate counts by adult role and channel, only in an approved system after activation: concept-page visits, voluntary adult replies, qualified replies, conversations, explicit pilot-interest signals, declines/opt-outs, and reported child-data attempts. Suggested day-14 learning thresholds: **20 relevant visits, 6 adult replies, 4 qualified conversations, 2 clear pilot-fit signals**. These are small-sample decision aids; missing them means revise the channel or proposition, not send more messages automatically. Do not infer unique people from page analytics or claim attribution without a verified source.

Qualify an adult reply only when: (1) the respondent is an adult parent/guardian, trumpet tutor, band educator, or program lead; (2) they describe a real beginner practice context in broad terms; (3) they can identify one friction or critique; (4) they voluntarily agree to a conversation or future pilot update; and (5) no child identifier or unauthorized institutional commitment is needed. A page visit alone is not a lead. An adult mailto draft is not consent until the adult sends it, and a reply is not blanket permission for follow-ups.

Stop immediately for a decline, opt-out, group-rule conflict, child data, request to contact a minor, unverified partnership claim, school approval requirement, provider action, unexpected cost, or evidence that the practice flow conflicts with teaching. Quarantine and delete child identifiers from any operational notes under a separately reviewed retention procedure; this package stores none. One nonresponse follow-up maximum, then stop.
