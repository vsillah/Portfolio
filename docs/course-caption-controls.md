# Course caption controls

Agentified and Accelerated share one caption size control. Show three `Aa` samples at the actual small, medium, and large caption scales (currently 14, 17, and 21 px), so learners can preview each choice before selecting it. Use `Small captions`, `Medium captions`, and `Large captions` as the button names, and `Caption size` as the accessible group name. Keep one clearly selected choice, keyboard-operable buttons, and the shared browser preference across lessons and workshops.

Keep the control compact at 390 px. The samples carry the visible meaning; a separate `Size` label is unnecessary. Hover must remain visually distinct from selection. Caption off/on, cue timing, native track modes, player geometry, and persistence must continue to work at every size.
