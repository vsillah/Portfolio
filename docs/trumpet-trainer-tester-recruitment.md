# Trumpet Trainer: founding-family tester recruitment kit

Status: internal draft for review. No list import, provider setup, message, post, schedule, or enrollment is authorized by this document.

## Offer and boundaries

The page at `/work/trumpet-trainer` describes a child-friendly beginner trumpet practice concept. The pilot is exploratory and limited. Adults may indicate non-binding interest through an email draft they review and send themselves. The Portfolio contact endpoint was deliberately not reused: it stores leads and triggers qualification automation. A production intake needs separate privacy, retention, consent, and activation review. The page does not collect child names, schools, exact ages, photos, addresses, or recordings.

There is no checkout, paid entitlement, school approval, performance promise, or guarantee that image-assisted music reading works. Pricing conversations are research, not offers.

## Target list criteria

Use only adults with a relevant, legitimate relationship or publicly listed professional contact channel. Prioritize (1) parents or guardians of beginner trumpet students who voluntarily opt in, (2) private trumpet tutors, (3) band educators, and (4) community music programs. Record the source, relationship, permission basis, role, date, and opt-out state before any approved outreach. Exclude minors, scraped personal contacts, student rosters, child data, and inferred family details.

## Sequence and weekly cadence (proposal)

| Week | Work | Decision signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Review page, pilot scope, privacy copy, outreach list rules, and messages with the Captain. | Written approval for the exact channel and bounded recipients. |
| 1 | If approved, invite a very small group of known adult contacts and publish one reviewed LinkedIn post. | Replies, qualified interest, objections, and unsubscribes. |
| 2 | Hold short adult discovery conversations; send one respectful follow-up to nonresponders only if the approved channel permits it. | Fit and willingness to test. |
| 3 | Review patterns and decide whether to open a pilot, revise the concept, or pause. | Evidence of repeated practice friction and a workable consent path. |

No automated sequence is proposed. Stop contact when someone declines, opts out, or does not fit. Do not treat an email draft or page visit as consent to future messages.

I am shaping Trumpet Trainer around short, clear practice steps and looking for a few adults willing to tell me where that idea helps or falls short. If you support a beginner trumpet student, would you be open to reviewing the concept? There is no purchase or commitment. Please share no child details by reply. [Portfolio page]

## Screening questions for an adult conversation

1. Which role best describes how you support beginner trumpet practice?
2. How long have the students you support generally been learning? Use broad ranges only.
3. What usually makes a practice session hard to start?
4. What does the adult currently do when the student gets stuck?
5. Which part of the concept seems useful, and which part would you remove?
6. What would you need to know before trying a short pilot? Ask about consent and access without requesting child identifiers.
7. Would you be open to a follow-up about a pilot if one is approved? Record the adult's answer and preferred channel.
8. Optional price research: if the tool proved useful, what would make it worth paying for, and who would decide? Do not quote a price or take payment.

## Simple measures

Track approved adult invitations, page visits where available in aggregate, replies, qualified adult interest, discovery conversations, pilot invitations, accepted invitations, completed feedback, and opt-outs. Separate each count by channel and role. Keep notes in the approved system only; report themes in aggregate. Do not build student profiles from this research.

## Activation gate

Before a durable tester intake or outreach campaign: Captain reviews the exact form destination, retention, consent language, access controls, suppression handling, recipient list, message copy, channel, and pilot capacity. An adult authentication and billing decision belongs to the Trumpet Trainer product lane. No Portfolio Supabase mutation is part of this PR.
