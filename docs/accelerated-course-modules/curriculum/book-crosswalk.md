# Book to workshop crosswalk

The canonical sequence is the EPUB table of contents, checked against its spine and chapter body with EbookLib. The book has a foreword, a data-safety note, and 26 numbered chapters. Its printed part markers are **Part I: Systems** (before Chapter 1), **Part II: Align** (before Chapter 5), and **Part III: Momentum** (before Chapter 17). The teaching overlay calls these Signals, Alignment, Momentum; that overlay does not rename the book's chapters.
