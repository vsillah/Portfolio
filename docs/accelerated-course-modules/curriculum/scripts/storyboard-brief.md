# L01–L04 storyboard brief

This is a shot handoff, not a media-generation request. Source art is the already approved Portfolio asset. The synthetic documents and cards below must be built as new, privacy-safe instructional graphics; they are not customer or product captures. Keep spoken narration verbatim from each lesson file, with captions checked against the eventual final mix. The dominant frame is 16:9. Reserve 10% on all edges for captions and mobile crop; text never sits inside the bottom 18%. For 9:16, reflow cards into a vertical stack instead of cropping body text. Use a still hold instead of the indicated motion when reduced motion is requested. No rapid flashes. Do not put avatar over a worksheet, chapter label, or art text. The infographic has dense lettering: use the full image for orientation only, then use an enlarged panel and adjacent accessible labels. Its printed quote and tool names are book art, not current product claims.

## Editorial and accessibility checks before production

- The storyboard designer must preserve exact lesson scene boundaries. Any retime that changes spoken rate or avatar window returns to editorial review.
- A1 and A2 must remain intact source assets. Cropping may emphasize a panel or target, but no new master SAM diagram or implied product outcome may be drawn.
- Synthetic screens must look like instructional examples, never like authentic captured customer evidence. The narration supplies the distinction without production jargon on screen.
- A public Portfolio course/prototype screenshot remains blocked until an exact route, revision, capture date, rights and privacy review, and asset hash are logged. No such capture is required to storyboard these lessons.
