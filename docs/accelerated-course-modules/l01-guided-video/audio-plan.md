# L01 audio plan

The pilot animatic is silent. It contains no generated voice, avatar, music, or effects. Its caption timing is a storyboard estimate based on approved text and scene duration; it is not a transcript synchronized to recorded speech.

Final production would use one narrator track across 09:00. A01 (00:00–00:35), A02 (03:55–04:15), A03 (05:45–06:05), and A04 (08:35–09:00) are reserved avatar windows. If native avatar audio is later approved, it replaces the narrator audio for those exact windows. No double speech. Music is optional and must sit below speech; worksheet holds remain free of effects.

After a real recording, align captions by ear to the final mix, identify meaningful sound, verify technical terms, and recheck both the 16:9 and portrait-safe caption band. Compare source and composite avatar frame count, frame rate, duration, and sync. Preserve the native source MP4 and reproducible FFmpeg command. No audio asset or provider setting is approved in this pilot.
