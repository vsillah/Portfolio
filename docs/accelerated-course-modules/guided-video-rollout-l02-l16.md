# Accelerated guided-video rollout: L02–L16

L01 is the only finished guided video. The remaining lessons have editorial scripts and visual production packets. Their learner-facing video controls stay locked until each finished file has its own approved source, composite, captions, and QA receipt.

## Reusable composition contract

Use L01's native composite as the *method* reference, not as footage or a timing template. Each lesson needs a frame-exact timeline, scene art, presenter windows placed in clear space, a single spoken owner at each point, native source speed and aspect ratio, and a content-aligned handoff after each presenter window. Keep the learner video at a stable 16:9 frame and provide an English VTT, SRT, and read-along text from the final audio. Preserve the approved SAM and book assets at natural aspect ratio. Keep synthetic examples labeled and private material out of the render.

The player contract is a versioned local MP4 and VTT path, displayed duration, native playback controls, visible caption control, transcript, return to the Learning plan, and a locked state for lessons without approved assets. Preview progress remains a learner-controlled local state; watching a video does not silently mark a lesson complete.

## Required packet per lesson

For every L02–L16 lesson, record: lesson ID and title; approved script revision and book crosswalk; approved visual and SAM asset hashes; scene-by-scene timing and presenter windows; authorized voice/likeness identity and usage rights; provider request and cost cap if a provider is proposed; native source job ID and SHA-256; audio ownership map; caption source and final VTT/SRT hashes; output MP4 hash, duration, frame rate, frame count, and codec; composite command and inputs; privacy review; rendered desktop/mobile evidence; and Human QA decision. Missing fields keep the video locked.

The existing script manifests and visual packets under `curriculum/scripts/` and `visual-production/` are planning inputs. Their `editorial-review` state is not a media approval.

## Batches and gates

| Batch | Lessons | Preparation | Gate before the next batch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | L02–L04 | Finalize scripts, evidence boundaries, scene timing, and media packets. | Editorial and source approval for each lesson. |
| 2 | L05–L08 | Reuse the verified composition contract; review the Signals-to-Alignment transition and the ten-part learning plan visuals. | Per-lesson decode, caption, accessibility, privacy, and Human QA. |
| 3 | L09–L12 | Prepare stakeholder, decision-loop, and synthetic-signal scenes with source labels. | Same per-lesson QA; stop on unsupported claims or missing rights. |
| 4 | L13–L16 | Prepare strategic-shift, influence, governance, and closing scenes. | Same per-lesson QA and an end-to-end sixteen-lesson navigation sweep. |

Within each batch, prepare scripts, visual maps, and offline composition checks together. Bind and unlock one lesson at a time only after its own receipt passes. Do not infer readiness from another lesson in the batch.

## Authorization boundaries

Offline planning, source mapping, test fixtures, and local draft renders can be prepared without a provider. Before any HeyGen or other provider request, obtain explicit current authorization for the exact lesson, approved script and avatar/voice identity, rights, provider job scope, enforceable cost cap, and stop conditions. A separate decision is needed for upload, deployment, public release, distribution, or any production-data use. A successful L01 review does not authorize any of those actions for L02–L16.

No L02–L16 video, avatar, narration, or caption media was generated by this rollout plan.
