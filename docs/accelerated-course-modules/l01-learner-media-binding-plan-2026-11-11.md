Copy only to a new immutable content-hash directory under `public/accelerated/lessons/l01/` or `public/accelerated/visual-parity/l01/`; never overwrite `approved-ee09bf72` or `visual-a70ed876`. Do not run `scripts/qa/stage-accelerated-visual-parity.py`: it moves all sixteen prior visual-parity outputs and rewrites the complete binding file.
2. **Update all three player contracts atomically.** `app/service-prototypes/[slug]/AcceleratedLearningPlan.tsx` takes the actual `<source>` from `lib/accelerated-visual-parity-bindings.json`, while `l01Media` supplies the caption track, duration label, and transcript. `lib/accelerated-end-screen-bindings.json` supplies the source/caption identity, final caption end/text, and poster for the end-screen gate. Update the L01 entries and their cross-check together; retain L02–L16 unchanged. A lone MP4 path change would leave old captions, transcript, and a blocked or mistimed end screen.
3. **Resolve the caption accessibility choice first.** The approved MP4 already displays all 175 cues in pixels. The current `<track default>` and `captionsOn=true` would overlay the old 84-cue VTT, producing duplicate, stale captions; disabling the track without changing the button would make “captions off” misleading because raster captions cannot be turned off or customized. Preferred production path: render a caption-free visual master from the same approved, source-bound material, derive a 175-cue WebVTT and read-along JSON from `caption-plan.json`, then obtain new exact-hash visual/audio and Human QA approval for that different MP4. If an always-visible raster master is instead chosen, record an explicit accessibility/product decision and provide an equivalent timed text track and truthful controls; do not claim the current approval covers that binding. In either path, verify cue words and boundaries against the final audio, speaker/source identity, end at 05:45 or earlier, mobile caption safety, keyboard/screen-reader access, and no duplicate text.
4. **Reconcile time and progress.** `lib/accelerated-decision-file.ts` lists L01 as 9 minutes and drives each row and the 161-minute course total. Decide whether “planned teaching” stays a curriculum estimate or becomes exact runtime; if runtime-based, render L01 as 05:45 and the course sum as 157:45 (157.75 minutes) rather than an awkward `5.75 min`. Update duration assertions and the end-screen rule in `lib/accelerated-end-screen-bindings.test.ts`, which currently requires final caption end to equal integer `minutes * 60`. The Learning Plan persists `videoSeconds` for guest and member progress. Its `restoreVideo()` currently seeks to any saved positive position without checking new duration, so an old L01 position of 345–540 seconds can seek past the new end. Use a media-hash-bound resume position or an explicit L01-only reset/clamp policy on load; preserve worksheets, completion, and other lesson progress. Test guest local storage, member fetch/save, pause/seek, and rollback behavior. Any member-data rewrite needs its own approved scope.
