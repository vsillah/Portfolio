## `AGT-IG-02`: AMINA is the operating loop

Phase: teach
Recommended Instagram format: carousel
Facebook companion: condensed Page post that explains the acronym
CTA: save/share, no release link required unless final calendar timing calls for it

### Instagram caption

```text
AMINA is the operating loop I want around agentic work:

Align the job.
Map the source.
Instrument the receipt.
Negotiate the boundary.
Audit the result.

The point is simple.

Before an agent moves work into the world, the system should know what it was asked to do, what it used, where it had to stop, who approved the next step, and what changed after the work moved.

That is how speed becomes reviewable.

Save this if your team is trying to move from AI demos to AI operations.

#AIProduct #ProductManagement #AgenticAI #AmaduTownAdvisory
```
