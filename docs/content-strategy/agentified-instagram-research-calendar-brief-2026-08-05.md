## Boundary

This brief extends the existing Agentified calendar into Instagram planning. It does not create a new strategy store, change campaign dates, seed live rows, call Meta, create provider drafts, schedule, publish, upload media, or store credentials.

Allowed:

- reuse approved Agentified, YouTube, LinkedIn, and X source packets;
- research public Instagram Reels, carousel, caption, and launch-pattern examples;
- prepare internal Instagram post, Reel, and carousel review packets;
- map each packet back to the shared Agentified calendar phase;
- record source-distance, rights, privacy, and human-review requirements.

Blocked until separate approval:

- Meta provider setup or production configuration;
- access-token entry, refresh, display, or storage;
- provider draft creation;
- external scheduling or posting;
- paid promotion;
- use of private analytics or non-public creator data.

## Calendar mapping

Instagram should serve the same four Agentified phases already used by LinkedIn, YouTube, and X.

| Phase | Instagram format | Calendar job | Required gate before final handoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tease | Single image or short Reel | Make the speed-versus-trust tension visible in one frame. | Visual asset and caption review. |
| Teach | Carousel | Teach AMINA through one move per slide. | Source-distance, rights, and privacy review. |
| Proof | Carousel or Reel | Show the receipt path without exposing private systems. | Asset packet approval and public-safe proof check. |
| Offer | Reel plus carousel companion | Point to `/agentified` only after the final offer path is approved. | Final copy, visual, provider config, and human submit gates. |

Use `instagram_reels` only when the calendar item needs motion or avatar/B-roll support. Use the existing Social Content provider value `instagram` for governed publishing readiness. Do not create a second Instagram strategy table.

## Public-safe research requirements

Amina should collect pattern evidence from public Instagram surfaces only. Each candidate needs:

- public URL or profile/post reference and retrieval date;
- creator/persona category;
- format: Reel, carousel, single image, caption-led post, or launch sequence;
- observable pattern: hook, first-frame structure, slide rhythm, proof style, CTA, caption shape, or comment prompt;
- fit for Agentified's target avatar;
- source-distance boundary;
- rights/privacy notes;
- Agentified calendar row or phase linkage;
- confidence state: `directional_signal`, `directional_insufficient_sample`, or `ready_for_review`.

If public metrics are hidden, thin, or platform-gated, mark the evidence `directional_insufficient_sample`. Do not invent performance claims.

## Transferable pattern boundaries

Transfer these:

- first-frame tension;
- before/after workflow framing;
- carousel rhythm;
- proof artifact sequencing;
- caption question style;
- release-day CTA structure;
- visual pacing for Reels.

Do not transfer these:

- creator wording, catchphrases, jokes, or signature phrases;
- visual identity, typography, templates, colors, or screenshot composition;
- private comments, DMs, subscriber-only posts, paid course material, or non-public analytics;
- unsupported claims about results, revenue, safety, or adoption;
- any private Portfolio, Chronicle, Open Brain, or customer records.

## Human gates

Instagram remains blocked until all of these are true:

- final copy/caption is approved;
- image, carousel, or Reel asset exists;
- visual asset packet is approved;
- privacy, rights, and redaction review is clear;
- Professional Instagram account and Meta Page linkage are confirmed;
- access token and IG user/business account ID are configured through the approved secret/config path;
- app review and required Meta publishing permissions are confirmed;
- Vambah approves final Instagram submission as its own action.

Provider configuration is readiness, not posting approval. The final Instagram publish action remains separate from research, copy review, asset creation, and provider setup.

## Review standard

Before any Instagram item moves toward final handoff, the packet must answer:

- What Agentified phase does this serve?
- Which approved source packet supports it?
- What public Instagram pattern informed it?
- What was adapted, and what was left alone?
- What visual asset is required?
- What rights/privacy check was completed?
- What remains blocked?
- What exact human decision is being requested?

A strong Instagram lane should make the idea easier to inspect, not harder to govern. The proof has to travel with the post.
