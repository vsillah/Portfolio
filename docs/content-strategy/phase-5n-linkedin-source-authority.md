## Evidence and measurement

- **Native metric:** Use only a directly reported LinkedIn impression count as the engagement-rate denominator. LinkedIn calls its impression count an estimate; the approved Phase 5N clarification permits that platform-reported count. A Portfolio-derived, modeled, reconstructed, back-calculated, or reach-substituted count is ineligible. Keep impressions, members reached, network percentages, follower gains, profile viewers, reactions, comments, reposts, saves, sends, and button engagement as separate fields. Do not add an absent metric or treat zero as absent. [Combined post analytics](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a701208).
- **Window:** Bind each post's actual publication timestamp and the exact start and end of a complete seven-day decision window. A 365-day dashboard range or lifetime total is not a seven-day post observation. The November 20 candidate observed November 26 was immature. API requests must specify both `dateRange` bounds; omitting them yields lifetime analytics. [Member post statistics](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/community-management/members/post-statistics?view=li-lms-2026-02).
- **Format and cohort:** Keep LinkedIn `text`, `image`, `document`, and `video` distinct. Require at least five distinct included comparables with the same format, audience band, metric definition, and cohort promise class. Review every excluded row explicitly. The November 1 chess post needs a topic/promise decision; excluding it leaves four of the five proposed comparables.
- **Baseline:** Each post needs its own attributable same-member, same-format baseline with at least five bound contributions and valid input/receipt hashes. The observed 898 followers can identify an audience band at that observation time; it is not a historical audience value for every older post.
- **Source policy and authority:** Bind the exact current source-class policy ID/version, evidence version, issued and explicit expiry timestamps, SHA-256, and approval receipt from the owning store. Do not infer expiry from capture date, provider availability, or a manifest string. Phase 5K's synthetic approval store does not approve LinkedIn historical evidence.
- **Integrity and rollback:** Keep original source bytes and their SHA-256 only in an approved private store for the permitted duration. Bind parsed fields, source path, schema/version, account/post IDs, collection and observation times, rights/privacy review, historical record ID, supersession, deletion or refresh date, and rollback procedure. A changed source requires a new version and approval; never silently rewrite an old receipt.

## Privacy, rights, and retention gate

Member analytics are internal/private. Keep individual viewer demographics, identities, comments, messages, connection lists, credentials, and unrelated profile data out of this calibration packet. Limit access to the account owner and approved reviewers. The member-operated export route still needs an explicit rights and retention decision for the proposed private store before use.

API data has additional restrictions. LinkedIn's [Marketing Developer Terms](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/l/marketing-api-terms) limit storage to what the developer documentation permits and require deletion in specified cases. Its [Data Storage Requirements](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/data-storage-requirements?view=li-lms-2026-11) distinguish field classes, including short limits on member social activity and profile data; the exact classification of each proposed analytics field and raw response has not been approved here. The [restricted-use guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/restricted-use-cases?view=li-lms-2026-02) also limits member-data use and transfer. Do not persist API raw bytes or derived member data until the project records a current field-level retention, deletion, consent, and permitted-use determination. If an immutable retention requirement conflicts with LinkedIn's allowed duration, the API route remains unavailable.
