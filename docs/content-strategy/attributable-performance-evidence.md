# Cross-channel attributable performance evidence

Phase 5F introduces an internal, read-only contract in `lib/attributable-performance-evidence.ts`. Each platform adapter binds the canonical object and account, exact platform format, publication and observation times, SHA-256 of the raw response, named metric and denominator, audience baseline, cohort, collector and scoring versions, source policy, provenance, rights clearance, and rollback record. Raw responses remain in the originating source store; this contract retains the digest and historical record ID. It does not fetch, persist, or execute anything.

Score only with at least five distinct, included comparables from the same platform, format, audience band, cohort, metric, and denominator. Preserve excluded records and reasons. Every weighted observation, including an external creator's, needs its own version-bound same-account baseline with a positive measured rate. Compare each observation's rate relative to its own baseline; never compare raw rates across creators. Missing denominators, ambiguous IDs, absent baselines, invalid source hashes, unsupported format equivalence, and unapproved or inferred expiry leave the receipt **Unscored**. Never replace missing relative performance with `1`.

The deterministic outlier score is the candidate's percentile rank among the matched, baseline-relative comparable rates, from 0 to 100. A score of at least **75** passes the internal evidence threshold; a scored receipt below 75 cannot prepare Human QA. The receipt includes a deterministic SHA-256 over the evaluation input and result. A directional or immature observation window cannot be used for decision-grade scoring.

Owned first-party analytics are preferred. External creator evidence gains performance weight only when the creator/account and object URL, attributable platform-authoritative source URL, metric, denominator, audience size, observation time, privacy/rights basis, and its own baseline are complete. Otherwise it is structural or topic context with zero performance weight. The baseline binds at least five contributing record IDs and raw hashes, verifies summed metric and denominator inputs, and carries input and receipt SHA-256 digests. An explicit `expires_at` must be issued by an approved source-class policy bound to the exact evidence version, platform, format, object ID, observation ID, and raw-response hash. The approval receipt and authority identity/version are included in its digest. The approval receipt itself must be verified by the owning approval store before use; this pure contract checks its binding. Retrieval, crawl, review, modification, and packet times cannot supply expiry. Historical observations remain retained after current eligibility expires.

Formats are separate: LinkedIn text/image/document/video; YouTube long-form/Shorts/Live; X single post/thread/image/video; Instagram feed/carousel/Reel; Facebook text/link/image/Reel/video. The existing cross-channel backlog declares a seven-day decision review; each platform/format adapter requires that full window. The 24–48 hour directional review stays Unscored. Neither scores nor rates are normalized across channels or formats.

A passing receipt may prepare an **internal Human QA artifact only**. It never approves copy, generates media, contacts a provider, uploads, schedules, publishes, posts, notifies, or overrides privacy, rights, Human QA, or external execution gates. Shadow replay uses synthetic fixtures and pure functions; live collection and production/staging writes are outside this phase.
