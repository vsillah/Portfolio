# Phase 5I: cross-channel shadow calibration

`lib/attributable-shadow-calibration.ts` is a pure, caller-driven review function over the merged 5F performance evaluator, 5G acquisition adapter, and 5H historical cohort constructor. It accepts checked-in synthetic or explicitly approved checked-in historical fixtures, exact outcome receipts, and a versioned reporting policy. It reads no files, environment values, credentials, network sources, or production rows. It writes nothing and enables no external action. The report retains every original 5H receipt, its nested 5F receipt, provenance, rights, rollback, inclusion and exclusion decisions, input hashes, and rejected labels.

## Supported approved outcome receipt

Every outcome must bind `case_id`, `artifact_version`, `evidence_version`, `platform`, `format`, exact `cohort_receipt_sha256`, exact `performance_receipt_sha256` (or null where 5H has none), `reviewer_or_authority`, unique `decision_receipt_id`, `decided_at`, `valid_until`, `privacy_cleared`, `rights_cleared`, `exclusions`, repository-relative `source_path`, and the self-bound `receipt_sha256`. Its `outcome` is exactly `pass`, `below_threshold`, or `unscored`; `unsupported_change` is an explicit reviewer classification. An outcome string or synthetic approval ID is not independent proof of authority. The owner of historical material must verify the actual decision receipt and privacy and rights authority before supplying a case. No label is promoted by this function.

Missing, duplicate, conflicting, stale, future-dated, cross-version, cross-channel, cross-format, wrong-hash, or ambiguous labels stay in the trace as unresolved and are excluded from calibration denominators. Duplicate case IDs also stay unresolved. A changed source response changes the 5H hash and rejects its prior label. Unknown-case outcome receipts are reported as orphans. The function never repairs or silently rebinds a label.

## Metrics and reportability

Reports group by the exact platform and format. YouTube long form and Shorts are separate; LinkedIn, X, Facebook, and Instagram retain their native formats. There is no cross-channel score, metric, denominator, audience, or threshold normalization. Each group reports sample size, resolved and unresolved label counts and rate, predicted Unscored count and rate, three-way predicted and approved counts, exact agreement and disagreement, explicit unsupported-change count and rate, fixture-kind coverage, and source/cohort rejection reasons. The unresolved-label rate counts missing or invalid approved labels. The predicted-Unscored rate counts deterministic 5F/5H Unscored outcomes across every case, regardless of label validity. They have separate denominators and policy limits. Binary precision and recall treat `pass` as positive and `below_threshold` as negative only when both the predicted and independently approved outcomes are scored. `unscored` rows never enter binary confusion counts. A zero precision or recall denominator is reported as null, never as zero or inferred confidence.

The supplied `CalibrationPolicy` must carry a policy ID and version, approval receipt ID, evaluation timestamp, minimum cases, minimum resolved labels, minimum independently scored positives and negatives, minimum valid binary comparisons, maximum unresolved-label rate, and maximum predicted-Unscored rate. Both maximum rates must be finite values in [0, 1]. An exact-limit rate passes; an above-limit rate gets its own blocker even when enough binary comparisons exist. Unavailable precision or recall denominators also block reporting. Invalid or unmet inputs fail closed to `reportable: false` and `confidence_eligible: false` for each group. These are reporting eligibility controls, not a change to the 5F outlier threshold. The receipt hashes the complete, deterministically ordered report. Rollback is discard the derived report and retain the original fixtures, labels, and historical receipts.

## Limits and future gate

The checked-in tests replay production-shaped synthetic records for LinkedIn, YouTube long form, YouTube Shorts, X, Facebook, and Instagram. They check ordering, source drift, label conflicts and staleness, wrong bindings, disagreement math, invalid denominators, insufficient sample size, and blocked network and environment access. The module imports only crypto and the pure 5F–5H contracts; it has no persistence, notification, label-promotion, provider, packet, or external-execution dependency. This lane creates no live collection path and performs no production preparation.

A threshold or playbook recommendation remains a review candidate until a separate, explicit policy decision identifies the exact version and affected channel/format, verifies historical authority and rights, evaluates impact with adequate independent samples, and authorizes a separate implementation and production validation. This function cannot change a threshold, playbook, label, campaign, calendar, packet, authorization state, or external lock.
