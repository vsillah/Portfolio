## Amina refinement recommendations

### `AGT-X-01`: What breaks first when AI gets faster?

Recommended format: short post with optional 3-reply thread.

Transferable pattern:

- tension-first hook;
- demo-to-production contrast;
- one practical question that invites operators to diagnose their own workflow.

Refinement direction:

- Keep the seed's "AI got faster. Trust did not." opening.
- Add one concrete line that names where the break happens: source, handoff, boundary, or approval.
- End with a question instead of a hard sell.

Human review focus:

- Confirm whether this should stay one post or become a short thread.
- Confirm whether `/agentified` CTA appears here or only in later rows.

### `AGT-X-02`: The operating layer behind AMINA

Recommended format: five-reply teaching thread.

Transferable pattern:

- framework decomposition;
- one operating move per reply;
- practical product-operator language rather than acronym performance.

Refinement direction:

- Spell out AMINA on first use: Align, Map, Instrument, Negotiate, Audit.
- Give each reply one question a leader can ask before allowing an agentic handoff.
- Avoid saying AMINA is "the answer." Present it as the operating loop around trust.

Human review focus:

- Confirm the acronym expansion.
- Confirm the level of specificity that is public-safe.

### `AGT-X-03`: The workbook is the receipt path

Recommended format: proof-artifact thread without private screenshots.

Transferable pattern:

- show the receipt path rather than only describing the philosophy;
- make proof concrete: source, role, boundary, gate, receipt, signal.

Refinement direction:

- Keep the workbook frame, but make the public claim conditional until the final release path is ready.
- Use approved cover/workbook language only.
- Treat this as the bridge from teaching into proof.

Human review focus:

- Confirm whether a public workbook visual is approved.
- Confirm whether the post can say the workbook is available or should say it is coming with the release.

### `AGT-X-04`: Agentified release thread: build trust before scale

Recommended format: final shout release thread.

Transferable pattern:

- launch-thread sequence: problem, operating method, proof, CTA;
- authorial voice rather than product-marketing voice.

Refinement direction:

- Anchor the thread on the final shout date.
- Make `/agentified` the only CTA unless the final page defines another route.
- Keep the promise grounded: build trust before scale.

Human review focus:

- Final CTA URL.
- X provider/API readiness.
- Post-now versus scheduled release timing.
