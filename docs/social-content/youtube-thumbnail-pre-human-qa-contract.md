# YouTube thumbnail pre-Human-QA contract

This checklist is mandatory before a YouTube thumbnail can reach Human QA.

## Editorial gate

Every candidate must have:

1. One dominant human or product focal point.
2. One idea readable in roughly one second.
3. No more than five large teaser words.
4. Thumbnail copy that adds tension or context instead of repeating the title.
5. A legible 320 x 180 feed-size preview.
6. A version-bound, spatial/layout-aware palette-and-contrast similarity score against the curated playlist. The playlist remains taste calibration only, with performance weight `0` and performance status `unscored`.
7. A fresh topic-specific public cohort with at least one threshold-qualified outlier.

## Pre-generation gate

A generation brief must pass before any HeyGen call. It must bind lighting and brightness target, palette, expressive campaign-relevant facial direction, face-prominence target, dominant focal point, teaser copy, title-copy contrast, owned product proof, current decision fingerprint, current live cohort, and current playlist-reference fingerprint.

Missing, stale, neutral, or incomplete fields produce `blocked`. `Unscored` never means approved.

## Post-render gate

The final raster must produce a version-bound passing quality receipt. Global averages are informational and cannot establish polish by themselves. The receipt also measures semantically bound foreground/background separation, WCAG text-panel contrast, subject/background separation, product-proof separation, dominant and accent palette strength, controlled palette count, background complexity, feed-size focal hierarchy, and spatial/layout-aware curated-playlist similarity. A separate post-generation visual-inspection receipt must bind the exact HeyGen source SHA, final-output SHA, and 320 x 180 feed-preview SHA. That receipt records the semantic sampling regions, face prominence, both visible unobstructed eyes, an expressive campaign-relevant expression, the ordered 320 x 180 focal hierarchy, and an actual transcription of the teaser from the rendered 320 x 180 preview. The renderer cannot author or hard-code this assessment.

No teaser text, logo, badge, product proof, border, or other overlay may cover or crowd the face. The inspection receipt must bind one face bounding box, every overlay bounding box, and at least 24 pixels of face-clearance padding to the exact final-output hash. Any padded-box intersection blocks the candidate.

Pastel washout, weak text contrast, low subject or product separation, visually busy backgrounds, uncontrolled palettes, dark or muddy treatment, neutral expressions, hidden eyes, undersized faces, stale evidence, repeated title copy, or a failed feed-size preview block screenshots, MP4s, and Human-QA packets.

Superseded or rejected artifacts remain labeled as failed evidence. They cannot be relabeled as passing by a later screenshot or wrapper receipt.
