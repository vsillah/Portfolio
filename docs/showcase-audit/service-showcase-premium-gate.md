# Service showcase premium gate

Apply this checklist before adding or replacing a service gallery frame. The first frame should work as a credible service landing-page opening; the second should show the buyer-facing experience or deliverable. A gallery is review material until Captain review and Human QA pass.

## Source and truth

- Match the active service ID and current listing image before binding a frame. Changed records fail closed.
- Search Portfolio sources, relevant AmaduTown Drive materials, and local project history before declaring a source gap. Record exact paths and what each source supports. Private material can guide structure, but cannot be quoted or placed in customer-facing art without an explicit basis.
- Label illustrative interfaces, lessons, progress, work products, and outcomes as illustrative. A poster is not a playable video. A local fixture is not a public deployment. Never invent client results, testimonials, integrations, or completed transactions.
- Preserve the approved Julie's Family Learning/KinFlo Website UX Refresh source pair and its `built example` label unless a later review explicitly changes it.

## Art direction

- Give each service a distinct composition, focal point, and visual rhythm. Keep AmaduTown's navy, gold, editorial serif, Inter, and full portrait shield without repeating a dashboard template.
- The primary frame needs a readable service promise, one strong focal image or visual idea, deliberate typography, and a clear mobile card crop. It should feel like a marketing splash page that could guide a real service page.
- The secondary frame must make the work concrete: a conversation and handoff, an operating path, a live learning exercise, a case curriculum, or a named course player and workbook. Use specific but synthetic content.
- Avoid generic admin chrome, fake analytics, mechanical arrows, repeated presenter imagery, tiny type, stock-looking cards, and filler copy. Keep disclosure legible without making it the visual headline.

## Review and release

- Render a contact sheet and inspect each full-size frame. Review the exact catalog card and detail route at desktop, tablet, and narrow phone widths. Check crop, labels, controls, price/actions, overflow, and both carousel states.
- Keep focused tests for 41 exact source-bound two-frame galleries, distinct hero assignments, the Julie source boundary, and the no-playable-video course boundary.
- Run the focused tests and build, then verify both Vercel preview contexts. Capture privacy-safe screenshots and MP4 from the latest commit-bound localhost route before requesting Human QA.
- Keep PR #1128 draft and unmerged until Captain review and the required client-facing Human QA gate.
