## Provenance and decisions

- Four digital compositions embed existing repo-local UI captures. The wide Lead Generation workflow is redrawn as a clearly labeled source-derived concept because a card-sized crop severed headings and nodes. The Diagnostic Template uses a synthetic, clearly labeled intake concept with empty fields because its original repository image contains operator and account data. Source images remain unchanged and are not copied into either generated concept asset.
- The six new service visuals and three enterprise contribution visuals say **Illustrative concept** in the image and card. They specify a possible buyer-facing experience; they do not certify an implemented workflow. The enterprise visuals use no Fidelity trademarks or account data.
- Dark Castle Chess and InclusivO were captured from their linked public pages on 2026-10-22. ReversR uses the approved AmaduTown Drive screenshot listed in its row, cropped for a wide card; its currency needs owner confirmation.
- ManifestR now uses a clearly labeled Studio-to-Canvas concept grounded in read-only inspection of the owner-controlled private repo and a local, provider-free Studio render. The concept reuses no private code, sample media, personal data, or generated output. The legacy linked AI Studio preview and live generation were not qualified.
- Homepage digital products and services now use a shared 5:3 gallery surface. Seven cards have two distinct, role-named frames: the Lead Generation Template has a workflow hero and an illustrative intake/review view; six service concepts have a hero plus a workflow, outcome, or course-preview poster. The other eight product/service cards have one defensible image and remain static. Each row's `carousel_items` records role, status, and provenance; `carousel_gap` explains the missing second source. New concept frames are illustrations, not evidence of live usage or client results.
- The self-paced course poster draws on the local Module 0 title card, script, and thumbnail brief. Its **Watch course preview** action remains disabled while `video_url` is absent. The card explains the recovery sequence: script review, separate render approval, playable file validation, then video binding. The poster is not a rendered lesson.
- Printful merchandise mockups, the three publication covers, and three existing service images remain unchanged. Their current public URLs are listed in the JSON snapshot.
- No public record or production asset was overwritten or uploaded. The overrides are version-bound to the exact current source URLs. Once the PR passes Human QA, merges, and deploys, the matching public cards change automatically; no database candidate approval is part of this code path.
